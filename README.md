# PowerBi-Project
🚀 Power BI Project Showcase | Mobile Sales Dashboard | End-to-End Data Analytics

Excited to share one of my latest Power BI dashboard projects focused on analyzing Mobile Sales Performance. This interactive dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales trends, customer behavior, and business performance through dynamic visualizations and KPI tracking.

📊 Project Highlights

This dashboard enables users to analyze:

Overall sales performance across different brands, cities, and mobile models.
Month-to-Date (MTD) sales and comparison with the Same Period Last Year (SPLY).
Sales trends by year, quarter, month, and day.
Customer ratings and transaction distribution by payment method.
Geographic sales analysis across major Indian cities.
Interactive filtering by Year, Month, Brand, Mobile Model, and Payment Method.
📌 Key KPIs

✅ Total Sales: 769M
✅ Total Quantity Sold: 19K Units
✅ Average Selling Price: 40.11K
✅ Total Transactions: 4K

🔍 Key Business Insights
Sales generated ₹769M from over 19K mobile units.
iPhone SE, OnePlus Nord, and Galaxy Note 20 emerged as the top-selling models.
Monthly sales peaked during July, indicating seasonal demand.
Customer ratings were predominantly Good, reflecting strong customer satisfaction.
Payment methods were evenly distributed across UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, and Cash, highlighting diverse customer preferences.
City-wise analysis identified high-performing markets, enabling targeted sales strategies.
